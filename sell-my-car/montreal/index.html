The paperwork in Quebec

Quebec transfers happen at the SAAQ, and both the seller and buyer generally need to be present. Quebec sales tax is calculated on the higher of the agreed price or the vehicle's estimated value, so an unusually low sale price does not reduce the buyer's tax bill.

Local tip. Because the SAAQ needs you both there, agree the appointment slot when you accept the bid — not after.

A plain-language summary, not legal advice. Requirements and tax rates change — confirm the current rules with Quebec before you close.
