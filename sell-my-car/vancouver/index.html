The paperwork in British Columbia

Transfers in British Columbia go through an Autoplan broker, where ICBC handles registration and insurance together. Both parties complete the transfer/tax form, and the buyer pays provincial sales tax at the counter on private sales — the rate steps up on higher-value vehicles.

Local tip. Book the Autoplan appointment with the winning bidder rather than leaving them to it. Deals that stall in BC almost always stall at the broker's office.

A plain-language summary, not legal advice. Requirements and tax rates change — confirm the current rules with British Columbia before you close.
