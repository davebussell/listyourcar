How it works

Four steps, one week, no haggling.

In detail
01

Check the number

Our Smart Estimate shows what competitive bidding should get you — and what a trade-in would cost you instead.

02

Set your terms

Pick a closing time and a reserve. Your reserve is the floor: below it, the car simply doesn't sell.

03

Let them compete

Verified dealers and private buyers bid in the open. Every bid is visible, and each one pushes the last.

04

Close and hand over

At your closing time the high bid wins. Clear the reserve and it's a sale — you deal directly with the buyer.

Smart Estimate

Know the number before anyone offers you one.

Every trade-in conversation starts with the dealer knowing more than you do. This flips it.

Feed in the year, make, model, distance and condition. You get three figures: what a dealer would hand you on trade, what competitive bidding should land, and the private-sale ceiling. Plus every factor we used to get there — depreciation curve, distance against the Canadian average, body-type demand, and the season you're selling in.
