What is a Jeep Grand Cherokee worth?

A 2021 Jeep Grand Cherokee in good condition with average distance is worth about $27,700 on trade-in — but should fetch $30,550–$34,100 when dealers and private buyers bid against each other. That gap is roughly $4,650.
