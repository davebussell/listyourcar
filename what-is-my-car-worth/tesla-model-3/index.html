What is a Tesla Model 3 worth?

A 2021 Tesla Model 3 in good condition with average distance is worth about $24,850 on trade-in — but should fetch $27,400–$30,600 when dealers and private buyers bid against each other. That gap is roughly $4,150.

Where these numbers come from

Our Smart Estimate starts from the Tesla Model 3's delivered price when new, then applies a depreciation curve, your distance measured against the Canadian average, condition, body-style demand, how well Tesla holds value relative to the segment, and the season you're selling in.

Nothing is hidden: run your own car and the tool shows every factor and what it added or subtracted. It's an estimate, not an appraisal — service history, accident record and tires all move the real number.
