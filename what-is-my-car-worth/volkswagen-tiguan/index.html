What is a Volkswagen Tiguan worth?

A 2021 Volkswagen Tiguan in good condition with average distance is worth about $18,650 on trade-in — but should fetch $20,600–$22,950 when dealers and private buyers bid against each other. That gap is roughly $3,150.
