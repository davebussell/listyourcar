What is a Ram 1500 worth?

A 2021 Ram 1500 in good condition with average distance is worth about $31,750 on trade-in — but should fetch $35,000–$39,100 when dealers and private buyers bid against each other. That gap is roughly $5,300.

Where these numbers come from

Our Smart Estimate starts from the Ram 1500's delivered price when new, then applies a depreciation curve, your distance measured against the Canadian average, condition, body-style demand, how well Ram holds value relative to the segment, and the season you're selling in.

Nothing is hidden: run your own car and the tool shows every factor and what it added or subtracted. It's an estimate, not an appraisal — service history, accident record and tires all move the real number.
