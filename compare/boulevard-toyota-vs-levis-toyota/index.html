Selling and buying

What trading in here is worth

In Quebec, GST and QST on a dealer sale is charged on the price minus your trade-in. So trading in at the store you buy from saves 14.975% of the trade-in's value in tax: on a $15,000 trade-in, about $2,246. A cash offer for your car from anyone else has to beat the trade-in by that much to leave you better off.

Tax saved by trading in: $2,246. To beat it, a cash offer must be over $17,246.

Common questions

Is Boulevard Toyota or Lévis Toyota better for selling my car?

On what their websites say, Lévis Toyota is the likelier buyer. In its favour: buyer's take; says it buys cars from the public; a page for selling or trading in. Get a written offer from both before you decide.

Is Boulevard Toyota or Lévis Toyota better for buying a Toyota?

Boulevard Toyota says more about what it offers buyers. Compare the price of the exact car you want at both, and check each one's reviews.

Which is open later, Boulevard Toyota or Lévis Toyota?

Boulevard Toyota: 4 weeknights to 8 pm or later, 56 showroom hours a week. Lévis Toyota: 4 weeknights to 8 pm or later, 53 hours a week. Hours as listed on their websites; holidays differ.

Should I trade in my car at Boulevard Toyota or Lévis Toyota, or sell it myself?

Trading in at the store you buy from saves 14.975% of the trade-in's value in sales tax, because tax is charged on the price minus the trade. A private sale usually gets more money, but it has to beat the trade-in by that margin to come out ahead.
