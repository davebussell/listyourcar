Selling and buying

What trading in here is worth

In Ontario, HST on a dealer sale is charged on the price minus your trade-in. So trading in at the store you buy from saves 13% of the trade-in's value in tax: on a $15,000 trade-in, about $1,950. A cash offer for your car from anyone else has to beat the trade-in by that much to leave you better off.

Common questions

Is Bytek Volkswagen or Hunt Club Volkswagen better for selling my car?

On what their websites say, Hunt Club Volkswagen is the likelier buyer. In its favour: online appraisal. Get a written offer from both before you decide.

Is Bytek Volkswagen or Hunt Club Volkswagen better for buying a Volkswagen?

Hunt Club Volkswagen says more about what it offers buyers. Compare the price of the exact car you want at both, and check each one's reviews.

Should I trade in my car at Bytek Volkswagen or Hunt Club Volkswagen, or sell it myself?

Trading in at the store you buy from saves 13% of the trade-in's value in sales tax, because tax is charged on the price minus the trade. A private sale usually gets more money, but it has to beat the trade-in by that margin to come out ahead.
