Selling and buying

What trading in here is worth

In Manitoba, GST and RST on a dealer sale is charged on the price minus your trade-in. So trading in at the store you buy from saves 12% of the trade-in's value in tax: on a $15,000 trade-in, about $1,800. A cash offer for your car from anyone else has to beat the trade-in by that much to leave you better off.

Tax saved by trading in: $1,800. To beat it, a cash offer must be over $16,800.
