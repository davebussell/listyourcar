Selling and buying

What trading in here is worth

In Alberta, GST on a dealer sale is charged on the price minus your trade-in. So trading in at the store you buy from saves 5% of the trade-in's value in tax: on a $15,000 trade-in, about $750. A cash offer for your car from anyone else has to beat the trade-in by that much to leave you better off.

Is Norden Volkswagen or West Edmonton Volkswagen better for selling my car?

They're level on what their websites say about buying cars. Get a written offer from both and take the higher one, or use it as your trade-in number.

Is Norden Volkswagen or West Edmonton Volkswagen better for buying a Volkswagen?

West Edmonton Volkswagen says more about what it offers buyers. Compare the price of the exact car you want at both, and check each one's reviews.

Which is open later, Norden Volkswagen or West Edmonton Volkswagen?

Norden Volkswagen: 3 weeknights to 8 pm or later, 63 showroom hours a week, open Sundays. West Edmonton Volkswagen: 4 weeknights to 8 pm or later, 72 hours a week, open Sundays. Hours as listed on their websites; holidays differ.

Should I trade in my car at Norden Volkswagen or West Edmonton Volkswagen, or sell it myself?

Trading in at the store you buy from saves 5% of the trade-in's value in sales tax, because tax is charged on the price minus the trade. A private sale usually gets more money, but it has to beat the trade-in by that margin to come out ahead.
