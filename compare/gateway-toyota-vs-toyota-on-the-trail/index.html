Selling and buying

What trading in here is worth

In Alberta, GST on a dealer sale is charged on the price minus your trade-in. So trading in at the store you buy from saves 5% of the trade-in's value in tax: on a $15,000 trade-in, about $750. A cash offer for your car from anyone else has to beat the trade-in by that much to leave you better off.

Common questions

Is Gateway Toyota or Toyota on the Trail better for selling my car?

On what their websites say, Gateway Toyota is the likelier buyer. In its favour: buyer's take; says it buys cars from the public; a page for selling or trading in. Get a written offer from both before you decide.

Is Gateway Toyota or Toyota on the Trail better for buying a Toyota?

Toyota on the Trail says more about what it offers buyers. Compare the price of the exact car you want at both, and check each one's reviews.

Which is open later, Gateway Toyota or Toyota on the Trail?

Gateway Toyota: 4 weeknights to 8 pm or later, 80 showroom hours a week, open Sundays. Toyota on the Trail: 4 weeknights to 8 pm or later, 82 hours a week, open Sundays. Hours as listed on their websites; holidays differ.

Should I trade in my car at Gateway Toyota or Toyota on the Trail, or sell it myself?

Trading in at the store you buy from saves 5% of the trade-in's value in sales tax, because tax is charged on the price minus the trade. A private sale usually gets more money, but it has to beat the trade-in by that margin to come out ahead.
