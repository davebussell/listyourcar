Selling and buying

What trading in here is worth

In Ontario, HST on a dealer sale is charged on the price minus your trade-in. So trading in at the store you buy from saves 13% of the trade-in's value in tax: on a $15,000 trade-in, about $1,950. A cash offer for your car from anyone else has to beat the trade-in by that much to leave you better off.

Common questions

Is Kitchener Honda or Waterloo Honda better for selling my car?

On what their websites say, Kitchener Honda is the likelier buyer. In its favour: buyer's take; a page for selling or trading in; online appraisal. Get a written offer from both before you decide.

Is Kitchener Honda or Waterloo Honda better for buying a Honda?

Waterloo Honda says more about what it offers buyers. Compare the price of the exact car you want at both, and check each one's reviews.

Which is open later, Kitchener Honda or Waterloo Honda?

Kitchener Honda: 4 weeknights to 8 pm or later, 60 showroom hours a week. Waterloo Honda: 4 weeknights to 8 pm or later, 60 hours a week. Hours as listed on their websites; holidays differ.

Should I trade in my car at Kitchener Honda or Waterloo Honda, or sell it myself?

Trading in at the store you buy from saves 13% of the trade-in's value in sales tax, because tax is charged on the price minus the trade. A private sale usually gets more money, but it has to beat the trade-in by that margin to come out ahead.
