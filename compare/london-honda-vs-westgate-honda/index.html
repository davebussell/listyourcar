Selling and buying

What trading in here is worth

In Ontario, HST on a dealer sale is charged on the price minus your trade-in. So trading in at the store you buy from saves 13% of the trade-in's value in tax: on a $15,000 trade-in, about $1,950. A cash offer for your car from anyone else has to beat the trade-in by that much to leave you better off.

Tax saved by trading in: $1,950. To beat it, a cash offer must be over $16,950.

On what their websites say, London Honda is the likelier buyer. In its favour: buyer's take; says it buys cars from the public. Get a written offer from both before you decide.

Is London Honda or Westgate Honda better for buying a Honda?

Westgate Honda says more about what it offers buyers. Compare the price of the exact car you want at both, and check each one's reviews.

Which is open later, London Honda or Westgate Honda?

London Honda: 3 weeknights to 8 pm or later, 50 showroom hours a week. Westgate Honda: 4 weeknights to 8 pm or later, 61 hours a week. Hours as listed on their websites; holidays differ.

Should I trade in my car at London Honda or Westgate Honda, or sell it myself?

Trading in at the store you buy from saves 13% of the trade-in's value in sales tax, because tax is charged on the price minus the trade. A private sale usually gets more money, but it has to beat the trade-in by that margin to come out ahead.
