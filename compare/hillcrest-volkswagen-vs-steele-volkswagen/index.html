Selling and buying

What trading in here is worth

In Nova Scotia, HST on a dealer sale is charged on the price minus your trade-in. So trading in at the store you buy from saves 14% of the trade-in's value in tax: on a $15,000 trade-in, about $2,100. A cash offer for your car from anyone else has to beat the trade-in by that much to leave you better off.
