Why it's better stock

One owner, full history, no auction-lane roulette.

Wholesale lanes are full of cars dealers already decided not to retail. This is the opposite pool: private owners who would otherwise have traded in, listing directly because the auction beats the trade-in number. You're seeing the car before it becomes someone else's problem.

Every listing carries the owner's own disclosure, distance, condition grade and location. You bid knowing what you're buying.
