About listyourcar.ca

Canada's marketplace for renting characterful cars as backdrops for photos, films and events.

listyourcar.ca connects people who own special cars — vintage classics, luxury marques, muscle, exotics and military vehicles — with the photographers, couples, families, and productions who want to shoot with them. Owners earn from their car without selling it; creators find the exact car their shot needs.

What makes us different

  • Cars you can't get at a rental counter — curated, characterful, camera-ready.
  • Browse by collection, by city, and by occasion (weddings, prom, events & more).
  • Book by the hour, the half-day or the day; the owner is on set.
  • Free to list — owners only pay when their car books.

Our story

Built in Canada, listyourcar.ca started with a simple idea: the most beautiful cars spend their lives parked, while creators spend days hunting for the right one to shoot. We bring the two together — turning standout cars into income for their owners and unforgettable frames for everyone else.
