About listyourcar.ca

List your car. Sell it, rent it, or both — from a single dashboard.

listyourcar.ca is Canada's two-sided car marketplace. Car owners are the supply side; buyers and renters are the demand side. An owner creates one vehicle profile and decides at the intent step whether to sell it, rent it, or do both — a single-listing, dual-intent model no other Canadian platform offers from one owner dashboard.

What makes us different

  • One listing works for both sale and rental.
  • Crosspost to AutoTrader, Kijiji, Facebook, Craigslist & more.
  • Every inquiry — any channel, buyer or renter — in one inbox.
  • Free to list; you only pay when it makes you money.

Our story

Built in Canada, listyourcar.ca started with a simple idea: getting value from your car shouldn't be complicated or expensive — whether you're selling it or renting it out. We're a small team building the most straightforward way to do both.
