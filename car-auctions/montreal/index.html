What the Montreal market looks like

A big, price-sensitive market with heavy winter wear on older stock — which means well-kept examples stand out sharply and bid up. Listing details in French widens your bidder pool.

How bidding works

01

Every bid is public

Amount, bidder type and timestamp. Dealers bid under their business name.

03

Fixed closing time

04

Direct handover

Win above reserve and you get the seller's details immediately.

Buying in Quebec: Quebec transfers happen at the SAAQ, and both the seller and buyer generally need to be present. Quebec sales tax is calculated on the higher of the agreed price or the vehicle's estimated value, so an unusually low sale price does not reduce the buyer's tax bill.
