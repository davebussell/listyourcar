The Winnipeg used-car market

They are spread across 2 communities, with the most in Winnipeg (87) and Headingley (3). Independent lots make up 38% of the market here — more than the 31% national figure, so there is more choice outside the franchised stores than in most of the country. Ford has the most franchised rooftops (5), followed by Buick (4) and Chevrolet (4).

Dealer groups with more than one rooftop here: Murray (3). A group's stores often share used inventory, so one call can cover several lots.

Before you buy from a dealer

Buying in Manitoba

Registration and insurance run through Manitoba Public Insurance at an Autopac agent, and provincial sales tax applies when ownership transfers. Ask the dealer for proof of its dealer permit before you sign.

Wherever you buy

Get a vehicle history report and a lien check before you pay — a car sold with money owing on it can be repossessed from the new owner. Ask for an independent pre-purchase inspection; a dealer that refuses one is telling you something. And compare the all-in price, not the monthly payment.
