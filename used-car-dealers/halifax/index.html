The Halifax used-car market

They are spread across 5 communities, with the most in Halifax (22), Dartmouth (21) and Bedford (3). Independent lots make up 45% of the market here — more than the 31% national figure, so there is more choice outside the franchised stores than in most of the country. Dealer groups with more than one rooftop here: O'Regan's (9), Steele (8). A group's stores often share used inventory, so one call can cover several lots.

Before you buy from a dealer

Buying in Nova Scotia

A car needs a valid Motor Vehicle Inspection to be registered and driven in Nova Scotia, so confirm the inspection is current or included before you buy. HST applies to dealer sales.

Wherever you buy

Get a vehicle history report and a lien check before you pay — a car sold with money owing on it can be repossessed from the new owner. Ask for an independent pre-purchase inspection; a dealer that refuses one is telling you something. And compare the all-in price, not the monthly payment.
