The Lethbridge used-car market

All of them are in Lethbridge itself. Franchised stores dominate: independents are 16% of dealers here against 31% nationally, so most used cars in Lethbridge are sold off a new-car dealer's lot. Cadillac has the most franchised rooftops (2), followed by Dodge (2) and Ford (2).

Dealer groups with more than one rooftop here: Davis (3), College (2), Murray (2). A group's stores often share used inventory, so one call can cover several lots.

Specialists in the area: budget (1), trucks (1).

Before you buy from a dealer

Buying in Alberta

Dealers must be licensed by AMVIC, the Alberta Motor Vehicle Industry Council, and must give buyers a mechanical fitness assessment on a used car. Alberta has no provincial sales tax, so GST is the only sales tax on a dealer sale.

Wherever you buy

Get a vehicle history report and a lien check before you pay — a car sold with money owing on it can be repossessed from the new owner. Ask for an independent pre-purchase inspection; a dealer that refuses one is telling you something. And compare the all-in price, not the monthly payment.
