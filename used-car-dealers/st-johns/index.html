The St. John's used-car market

They are spread across 2 communities, with the most in St. John's (23) and Mount Pearl (6). Franchised stores dominate: independents are 21% of dealers here against 31% nationally, so most used cars in St. John's are sold off a new-car dealer's lot. Chrysler has the most franchised rooftops (2), followed by Ford (2) and Kia (2).

Dealer groups with more than one rooftop here: Hickman (3), Steele (3). A group's stores often share used inventory, so one call can cover several lots.

Before you buy from a dealer

Buying in Newfoundland and Labrador

Registration runs through the province's Motor Registration Division. HST applies to dealer sales, and it is worth confirming the dealer is licensed with the province before you commit.

Wherever you buy

Get a vehicle history report and a lien check before you pay — a car sold with money owing on it can be repossessed from the new owner. Ask for an independent pre-purchase inspection; a dealer that refuses one is telling you something. And compare the all-in price, not the monthly payment.
