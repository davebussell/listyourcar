The Vancouver used-car market

They are spread across 12 communities, with the most in Vancouver (89), Surrey (57) and Richmond (47). Independent lots make up 42% of the market here — more than the 31% national figure, so there is more choice outside the franchised stores than in most of the country. Ford has the most franchised rooftops (14), followed by Honda (12) and Volkswagen (11).

Dealer groups with more than one rooftop here: OpenRoad (15), Applewood (8), Carter (6), Morrey (6), Wolfe (4), Boundary (2), Coastal (2), Signature (2). A group's stores often share used inventory, so one call can cover several lots.

Specialists in the area: performance (3), budget (2), trucks (2), premium (2), classic (2), imports (1).

Before you buy from a dealer

Buying in British Columbia

Dealers must be licensed by the Vehicle Sales Authority of BC. A licensed dealer has to disclose material facts about a car, including damage above a set dollar amount, and the Motor Dealer Customer Compensation Fund can cover buyers in some cases where a dealer does not.

Wherever you buy

Get a vehicle history report and a lien check before you pay — a car sold with money owing on it can be repossessed from the new owner. Ask for an independent pre-purchase inspection; a dealer that refuses one is telling you something. And compare the all-in price, not the monthly payment.

Common questions

How many used car dealers are there in Vancouver?

Our network lists 291 dealerships in the Vancouver area that sell used cars: 122 independent used-car lots and 169 franchised new-car stores, which also sell the trade-ins they take in.

Which parts of Vancouver have the most used car dealers?

Vancouver (89), Surrey (57), Richmond (47). Together those account for 66% of the dealers in the area.
