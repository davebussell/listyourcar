The Toronto used-car market

They are spread across 26 communities, with the most in Toronto (92), Mississauga (84) and Brampton (45). Independent lots make up 38% of the market here — more than the 31% national figure, so there is more choice outside the franchised stores than in most of the country. Chrysler has the most franchised rooftops (39), followed by Ford (31) and Hyundai (29).

Dealer groups with more than one rooftop here: Pfaff (5), Yorkdale (5), Alta (4), Woodbine (4), Boyer (3), Highland (3), Leggat (3), Serpa (3). A group's stores often share used inventory, so one call can cover several lots.

Specialists in the area: premium (10), performance (4), imports (3), trucks (2), budget (2), electric & hybrid (1), classic (1).

Before you buy from a dealer

Buying in Ontario

Every dealer in Ontario must be registered with OMVIC, the Ontario Motor Vehicle Industry Council. Advertised prices must be all-in — everything except HST and licensing — so a dealer cannot add fees at the desk that were not in the ad. Buyers from a registered dealer are also backed by a compensation fund if the dealer fails to deliver.

Wherever you buy

Get a vehicle history report and a lien check before you pay — a car sold with money owing on it can be repossessed from the new owner. Ask for an independent pre-purchase inspection; a dealer that refuses one is telling you something. And compare the all-in price, not the monthly payment.
